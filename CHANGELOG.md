# Changelog

Every release of Mycelium Primal, newest first. Each one's full plan is in [`.folio/`](.folio/README.md), and its QA record in [`docs/qa/`](docs/qa/).

## 1.0.0 — Open to everyone (2026-11-14)

Primal goes public: the packages on npm as `@mycelium-llc/primal-*`, the MIT license, a landing page with a starter and a fork guide, and an acceptance app built from Primal alone.

## 0.9.0 — Every element keeps its promise

2026-11-03. Every primitive, cell and surface documents its contract, kept as framework-independent data next to the code, with the documentation generated from it.

## 0.8.0 — Whole places to work in (shipped together in 0.9.0)

2026-11-03. Reference surfaces show how cells combine into complete journeys, so a new project starts from working places rather than parts.

## 0.7.0 — Things look like what they are (shipped together in 0.9.0)

2026-11-03. Content, information and layout components say what things are and how they stand, and the first cells do whole jobs end to end.

## 0.6.0 — You always know where you are (shipped together in 0.9.0)

2026-11-03. Moving around and opening things on top follow written rules: navigation only moves, each top layer keeps its claim, and focus is never lost.

## 0.5.0 — Every action answers (shipped together in 0.9.0)

2026-11-03. Actions and their feedback follow one written behaviour model, so every action is acknowledged, its outcome is specific, and its weight matches what it did.

## 0.4.0 — Magma by default (shipped together in 0.9.0)

2026-11-03. Primal's default primary is Mycelium Magma (#E61924) on the brand's ink and white, with a full palette derived from that one colour.

## 0.3.0 — Fields that never leave you guessing (shipped together in 0.9.0)

2026-11-03. Every native form element is covered by a field primitive with one contract: label, description, required or optional, disabled, read-only, error, keyboard.

## 0.2.0 — Plain HTML reads right

2026-10-20. Anything written as plain HTML or Markdown is readable, well spaced and accessible with no classes, and every element of the standard has a decided outcome.

## 0.1.0 — A base you can make your own

2026-10-20. Primal exists as a neutral foundation whose whole look is set by tokens, so it works as it is and any future Mycelium design system starts from it.
