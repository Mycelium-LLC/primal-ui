Mycelium Primal

The open-source design system by Mycelium. Design from first principles.

Primal is a design system you can use as it is. It's also the base every future Mycelium design system starts from, which is why its own look stays plain: Mycelium's Magma on ink and white, and little else. A product makes it its own by overriding tokens, never by editing components.

Built on promises

Every element is a primitive, a cell or a surface. What decides which is the promise it keeps, not its size or its markup.

Primitive
Part of a promise that isn't a whole job yet: a label, a field, a button, a heading.
Cell
One complete, predictable job: a newsletter signup, a search with its results.
Surface
Where cells serve a journey: a sign-in page, a settings area.
