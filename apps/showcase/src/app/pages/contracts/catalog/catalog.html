Button

Layer
Primitive
Promise
Trigger a recognizable action when activated.
Behaviour
Mouse, touch, Enter and Space; disabled; pending; feedback after action.
Context
Where a person can reasonably anticipate an action.
Relationships
The label names the action; placement and emphasis carry priority.
States
Default, hover, focus-visible, pressed, disabled, pending.
Accessibility
Native button semantics, accessible name, keyboard activation, visible focus.
Failure conditions
Never silently ignores activation, falsely implies completion, or allows a duplicate while pending.
