Page structure

Landmarks tell assistive technology where things are: the header, the navigation, the main content, the footer. Primal keeps their roles and sets only the spacing between them. A landmark never gets a box: space and alignment group the page.

What each landmark is for

header
The introduction of the page or of a section: its name, and often its navigation.
nav
A set of links that moves the person. It never filters, toggles or creates anything.
search
Where searching or filtering happens.
main
The content the page is for. Exactly one per page.
article
A composition that stands on its own: a post, a release note, a message.
section
A part of something, with its own heading.
aside
Related, but not the point: a note, a tip, a list of further reading.
footer
The closing of the page or a section: who, when, and the fine print.
hgroup
A heading with its subtitle, which sits close under it and steps back.
