Authentication

A gate, not a page of content: one narrow column, the product's name above it, one question at a time. Each of its three places has its own address, so a link from an email lands on the right one.

The journey

  1. Arrive at sign-in from a link or a signed-out page.
  2. Sign in with email and password; a mismatch says so and offers the reset, beside the form.
  3. Forgot? The reset asks for the email only, and says a link is on its way without saying whether an account exists.
  4. New here? Sign-up asks for three things, and ends at "check your inbox".

Next two moves, within reach at every step: switch between sign-in and sign-up, and reach the reset.

Try ada@example.com with correct horse to sign in; any other password shows the mismatch.
