List and detail

A list of things, and one of them in full beside it. The list keeps its place while you read; each item has its own readable address, so it can be sent to someone and opened cold. Without room for both, each is its own page, with a way back.

The journey

  1. Find a report in the list, narrowed by typing.
  2. Open it: the detail shows beside the list, and the address names it.
  3. Change it: rename it in place; the list follows.
  4. Delete it: it goes at once, with Undo; or create one, and it opens.

Next two moves, within reach from every item: rename it and delete it, beside its title; New Report sits above the list.
