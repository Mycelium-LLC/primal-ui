Multi-step form

A task too long for one screen, broken into steps the person can see. The step is in the address, so Back goes to the step before; what is typed is kept across steps; the last step shows everything once more, each part with a way to change it.

The journey

  1. Workspace: its name and address.
  2. Team: who to invite, or no one yet.
  3. Review: everything once more, each with Change; then Create Workspace.
  4. Done: the workspace is made, and its first moves are offered.

Next two moves, within reach at every step: Back (and the browser's Back), and the step after.
