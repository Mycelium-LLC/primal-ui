Settings

A settings area: the sections listed at the side, each its own address, so "Notifications" can be linked to. A switch saves the moment it changes and says so beside it; a form of several fields saves when you press Save Changes; the irreversible lives last, apart, behind a confirmation.

The journey

  1. Arrive at a section from a link, or pick one at the side.
  2. Change a setting: a switch saves at once; a form saves with Save Changes, and says "Saved" where you pressed.
  3. Leave: nothing is lost, because every change was saved as it was made.

Next two moves, within reach in every section: the other sections at the side, and Save Changes at the end of a form.
