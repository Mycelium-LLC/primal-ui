App shell

The frame every place in a product sits in, and the part that holds still: the bar across the top (the product, search, your account), the side navigation between the product's places, and the place itself. It lays itself out by its own width: narrow, the side navigation folds behind Show Menu, and search moves into it, so nothing is lost, only put away.

The journey

  1. Arrive in a place: the side navigation says which, by its fill and to a screen reader.
  2. Move to another place from the side; the bar and the navigation don't move.
  3. On a phone, Show Menu opens the navigation over the place, and choosing a place closes it.

Next two moves, within reach from every place: the other places at the side, and your account in the bar.
