Feedback

Feedback is how the system answers. Each part speaks from one place, with one meaning (The behaviour model): nobody picks a toast by taste.

Alerts

Place: alert. Something about this place is true, and stays true until it changes. It sits above what it is about, and says its tone with a mark and words as well as colour. It has no close button: a fact is changed, not dismissed.

A banner

Place: banner. The whole product is in a different state: offline, under maintenance, signed in as someone else. Edge to edge, above everything it is about.

Changes are kept on this device and sent when you're back.

The product, below its banner.

Toasts

Place: toast, the corner: the one raised voice. It says what just happened where you can no longer see it, holds the undo, and fades: five seconds, eight with an undo, and it waits while you point at it or tab into it. Never for a routine change (the button says "Saved"), a lasting fact (an alert) or an error to fix (beside the thing that failed).
