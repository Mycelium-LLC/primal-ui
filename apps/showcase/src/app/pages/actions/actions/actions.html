Actions

An action is something you press to make something happen. Its label says what, with your verb and an object: "Save Changes", never "Submit". Every action names its kind (Action kinds), and its kind decides how it behaves; its emphasis decides only how loud it looks.

Four treatments

Each is a promise. Use the primary once per place, for the one thing that happens here.

Primary
This is what happens here.
Default
You can do this.
Quiet
Also possible, less often. No fill until you point at it.
Danger
This takes something away. Its edge and words, never a primary fill.

It answers at once

Give a button its work, and it keeps the feedback contract by itself: busy the moment you press it, the same size while it works, deaf to a second press, then it says what happened, where you are already looking. Press it twice, fast.

"Not available, still reachable" uses aria-disabled: the button stays in the tab order, so its description can say why it can't be pressed yet. Prefer it to disabled, which the keyboard skips.

With an icon

An icon goes before the words and helps you find the action; the words still say what it does.

Icon buttons

An icon alone, for actions everyone knows by their mark, among others on a toolbar or in a corner. It still has a name, read aloud and shown when you point at it. Quiet by default.

A row of actions

Safe actions together, at the end, the main one last. A destructive one never sits among them: it goes apart, at the start, a section gap away, in the danger treatment. On a phone the row stacks, with the main action on top.
