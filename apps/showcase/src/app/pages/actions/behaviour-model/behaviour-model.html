The behaviour model

Behaviour is the design you can't see. Every message Primal gives is placed by three facts, and the facts pick the place: nobody picks a toast by taste. This page is the model in words; every feedback part says which place it speaks from.

Three facts

What is it?
An acknowledgement, an outcome, a failure, a fact that stays true, a question.
Who is saying it?
The person (their own action answering), the system, an agent working for them, or the outside world.
What does it want?
Nothing, a glance, a decision, or everything: the person cannot go on until they answer.

The feedback contract

Silence is a message. Every action answers in three layers:

  1. Acknowledgement, within 100 ms, before any work: the button is busy, the toggle is pressed.
  2. Outcome, specific: "Saved", "Deleted “Q3 revenue”", never "Success". A pause of about half a second between them reads as intentional.
  3. Channel, where the eyes already are: on the button, under the field, at the row. Only what happened out of sight goes to the corner.

The weight ladder

How loud an answer is follows what the action did. Shouting at routine things kills the important answer.

From the loudest to the quietest
What it did Its answer Example
Cannot be undone Impossible to miss: a gate before, the danger fill on its last step, and its outcome said in words. Delete Workspace
Made or took away something Clear, not interrupting: the thing appears or goes; a toast with Undo when it went out of sight. Create Report, Delete Report
Runs in the background Glance-sized, and it fades: "Started", its progress where it lives, a toast when it ends. Export Reports
Routine Acknowledgement only. No toast. Save Changes, a switch, a sort

Rules that hold everywhere

  • A failure shows on the thing that failed, never in a corner far from it.
  • A real question is asked once, then waits. Something that can be undone is never asked: it is undone.
  • An incident switches the whole vocabulary: a banner, and every action that can't work says why.
  • The last signal of a task gets the most care: it is what the person remembers.
  • Optimistic answers only for what almost never fails, never for money; if the bet fails, the correction is as clear as the premature success.
