Action kinds

Every action is one of six kinds, and each kind is decided once, here, in plain words: what stands before it (gate), what happens from the press to the end (sequence), how loud its answer is (weight), how it is taken back (reversibility) and what shows when it fails (error). Every Primal action names its kind, and behaves as its kind is written. Read this and you know how any product built on Primal behaves without opening a screen.
