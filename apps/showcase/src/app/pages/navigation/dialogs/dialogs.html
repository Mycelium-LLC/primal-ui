Dialogs

A dialog interrupts. The page behind it is covered and holds still, and the one thing to do is answer it. So it is kept for what earns it: a question the work cannot go on without. It always has a way out that isn't the answer (Close, Escape, a press on the backdrop), returns the page exactly as it was, and is not a place: it has no address, and Back never lands in it. On a phone it rises from the bottom edge.
