Disclosure

A disclosure is a summary you open for the detail: the browser's own <details>, so it works without script, is found by the browser's search when closed, and keeps its open state where it is shown. Opening one never moves what you are reading.

An accordion

Several, one under the other. Each opens on its own.

What is a primitive?

Part of a promise that isn't a whole job yet: a field, a button, a link.

What is a cell?

One complete, predictable job, built from primitives: a sign-in form, a newsletter signup.

What is a surface?

A place where cells serve a journey: a settings page, an app shell.

Only one open

Give them the same name, and opening one closes the others: for answers you compare, not read together.

Why does Primal mark optional fields, not required ones?

Most of a good form is required, so marking the few optional ones is less noise, and says what you can leave.
