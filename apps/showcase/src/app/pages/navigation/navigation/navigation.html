Navigation

Navigation only moves you. It never filters, toggles or creates: that is an action. Each part here is one of three moves: tabs switch between faces of one thing, breadcrumbs and navigation items go to a place, and pagination moves through one list.

The way up a real hierarchy, each step a link. Where you are is said in words, not linked.

Tabs

Faces of one thing: a report's summary, its data, its history. A face keeps what you did in it while you look at another: type a note, switch tabs, come back. Tab reaches the chosen tab; the arrow keys move between tabs and show each.

Pagination

One list, page by page. The first and last pages and the ones around yours are always there; the rest fold into a gap. Keep the page in the address so it can be shared and Back returns to it.
