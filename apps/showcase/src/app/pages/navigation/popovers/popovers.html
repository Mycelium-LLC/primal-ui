Popovers and tooltips

Both sit beside the page, not over it: the page stays yours, it scrolls and reads, and pressing outside closes them. A popover holds something: details, a choice, a small form. A tooltip is a short phrase about what you're pointing at, and never holds anything to press.

A popover

Opened by its button. If it holds something to press or type, focus moves into it; Escape or a press outside closes it, and focus goes back to the button.

Revenue counts paid invoices in the quarter, after refunds and before tax.

Trials and credits are left out.

Tooltips

On hover after a moment, and at once on keyboard focus. Escape hides one and leaves focus where it is. Tab to these, or point at them.

Nothing is ever only in a tooltip: on a phone there is no hover. The icon button's name is read aloud and shown when you point at it; the tooltip adds to it.
