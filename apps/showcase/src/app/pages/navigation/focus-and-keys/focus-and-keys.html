Focus and keys

The keyboard is a full way to use anything built on Primal, not a fallback. Four rules hold everywhere, and every component's keys are written down below.

Four rules

  1. Focus is always visible. One ring, in the primary, means one thing: this is where the keyboard is.
  2. Focus moves in reading order. Tab follows the page as it reads, in either direction; nothing jumps.
  3. Every layer has a return. When a dialog, popover, menu or tooltip closes, focus goes back to what opened it.
  4. Escape closes the topmost layer. One press, one layer; never the dialog under the menu.

Try it

Press Tab from here. Every stop shows the ring, in reading order. Open the menu or the popover, press Escape, and focus is back on its button. Switch the direction at the top to right-to-left and Tab again: the order follows.
