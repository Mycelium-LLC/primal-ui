Cells

A cell does one whole job, end to end, feedback included, built only from primitives. Each names its payload: the one thing it exists to show. When room runs out, the payload stays and everything else gives way.

Subscribe

Payload: the promise (what arrives, how often). The job ends in a subscription said in place, or an error under the field that says what to do. Try bounce@example.com to see the error.

Search with results

Payload: the results. While it waits it shows their shape, then how many and each with the match marked; with none it offers a way out; if search fails it says so in place. Search for field, zzz, or break.

An item tile

Payload: the issue's title. In a narrow tile (under 15rem) its number, labels and assignee give way; the title stays and wraps, never cut. These tiles are narrow on a desk, so only the state and title show; on a phone each is full width and the rest comes back.
