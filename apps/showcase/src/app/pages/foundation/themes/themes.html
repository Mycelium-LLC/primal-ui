Themes

Primal looks plain on purpose. It's the base that other design systems start from, so it carries no brand, and what you see here is exactly what a project gets when it imports Primal. A project gets its own look by overriding tokens in one file of its own, and no component changes.

The modes

Theme: light or dark
Follows the system, or is forced with data-theme.
Contrast: standard or more
Independent of the theme, so more contrast works in light and in dark. Follows the system, or is forced with data-contrast.
Density: comfortable or dense
The three gaps tighten, set with data-density.

Each mode can be set on the whole page or on one region of it, as in the regions below.
