Spacing

Space shows how things are grouped. Primal has three gaps: a large one between sections, a medium one between the elements of a group, and a small one inside a component. The gaps differ enough to see at a glance. Two things the same distance apart read as equals, so every gap is chosen on purpose.

Dense mode tightens all three, for screens people use all day. Switch Density in the header to see it here and everywhere else.
