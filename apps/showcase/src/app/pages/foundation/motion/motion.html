Motion

Motion in Primal is functional and quiet. It acknowledges a change, brings something in, or takes something away, and that is all. It's short, with no springs and no loops, and nobody ever has to wait for an animation to finish. A product adds its own character by changing these tokens.

If the system asks for reduced motion, every duration is zero and changes happen instantly. With reduced motion on, the demo below jumps instead of sliding.
