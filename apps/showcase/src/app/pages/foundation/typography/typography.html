Typography

Primal uses the system font, so it brings no typeface of its own. Sizes are fluid: each step grows smoothly between phone and desk widths with no jump at a breakpoint. Weight has two steps, regular and strong, so a heavier weight always means higher rank. Body text stops at a readable line length.

The measure

This paragraph is as wide as body text ever gets: about 68 characters. Longer lines make the eye lose its place when it jumps back to the start of the next one. Shorter lines break the reading rhythm. A wide screen gets more space around the text, not longer lines.
