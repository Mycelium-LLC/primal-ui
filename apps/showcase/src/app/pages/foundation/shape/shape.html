Radius, borders and elevation

A treatment is a promise. Every radius, line and shadow in Primal finishes the sentence "this style means: …". If a treatment can't finish it, Primal doesn't use it. Space does the grouping, so a box gets a line only when it's something you act on or something on top of the page.
