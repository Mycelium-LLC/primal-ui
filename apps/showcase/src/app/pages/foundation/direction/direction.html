Direction

Every component is laid out with logical properties: start and end instead of left and right, block and inline instead of top and side. That way the same styles work left to right and right to left. Switch Direction in the header and the whole showcase mirrors, including the navigation, the tables and the spacing.

When there's no logical form (a translate, for example), the right-to-left rule is written out and documented with the component that needs it.
