Colour

A project's colour comes from three choices: its black, its white and its primary. Its black doesn't have to be #000, and its white doesn't have to be #fff. Every other colour is derived from these three, in light and in dark, and stays readable. Primal starts from Mycelium's own: Magma on ink and white. Try your own below.

The primary

The primary keeps the colour you chose wherever it reads. Where it wouldn't, it is darkened in light or lightened in dark, keeping its hue, only as far as reading needs. Its states move towards the text colour: a step on hover, two when pressed.

Its scale

Eleven steps for illustration, charts and anything the roles don't name. Step 500 is your primary; the lighter steps mix it with your white, the darker ones with your black. Reach for a role first: a step says nothing about what it's for.
