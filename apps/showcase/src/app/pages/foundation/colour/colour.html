Colour

A project's colour comes from three choices: its black, its white and its primary. Its black doesn't have to be #000, and its white doesn't have to be #fff. Every other colour is derived from these three, in light and in dark, and stays readable. Try your own below.

Harmony

The companion and the tertiary come from the primary, a set distance round the colour wheel. Choose the harmony, and the distance follows.

System colours

Danger, warning, info and success keep their hues in every project, outside the harmony, so they always read as what they are. Each has a quiet background (--pr-color-danger-surface, and so on) mixed with the page.
