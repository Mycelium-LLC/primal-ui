Text fields

A text field is one question with a typed answer. It has a visible label, a description when the question needs one, and its message directly under it. The control stays the browser's own input, so autofill, the phone's keyboard for each kind and pasting all keep working.

Most of a good form is required, so Primal marks what's optional rather than what's required. Try leaving a field, or typing something it can't take: the message says what to type.

The kinds

Shaped as you type

Email, phone and website are shaped while they are typed, and when they are pasted: an email keeps no spaces and one @, with its domain in lower case; a phone number takes its separators from a pattern (primalMask="(000) 000-0000" for any other); a website starts with https://, written in for you. The caret stays where you are typing.

Search

A search box opens its list as soon as it has focus: empty, it shows what is popular; typing narrows it to what matches. Give the empty list its own options with [blank], and open it on focus with [openOnFocus]="true".

Numbers

A number is typed or stepped. − and + step by the field's step and stop at its min and max, faster while held; the arrow keys work as they do in any number field, and the unit sits after the value.
