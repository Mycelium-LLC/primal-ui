Validation

An error says what happened and what to type. It sits directly under its field, and it's announced when it appears.

The contract

When it appears
When the person leaves a field they've answered wrongly, or when they try to submit. Never while they're still typing their first try.
Where
Directly under its field, in the danger colour, and the field's edge turns the same colour. Never in a banner or a toast.
How it's announced
The message is read out as it appears, and again when the field is focused. On submit, focus moves to the first field to fix.
How it clears
As soon as the answer is right. The person doesn't have to submit again to see that it's fixed.
What it says
What to type, in the person's words, named after the field's label. The browser's own checks (required, email, length, range, pattern) decide what's wrong. Your forms library or your server can supply a message, and theirs wins.
