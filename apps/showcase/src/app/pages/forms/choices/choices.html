Choices

A choice is answered by pointing, not typing: a checkbox, a radio or a switch. The control comes first, then its label, and the whole label is the target, so the click lands anywhere on the words.

Checkboxes

Each checkbox is its own yes or no.

Some of a group

A checkbox that stands for a group shows a dash while only some of it is chosen.

A radio group

One answer from a few. The group is a single stop in the tab order, and the arrow keys move between its options, the way the browser does it. The group asks the question, and it holds the one message.

Switches

A switch changes a setting at once, with no Save. Its label says what it changes, never just "On" or "Enabled", so the person knows what happens before they flip it.
