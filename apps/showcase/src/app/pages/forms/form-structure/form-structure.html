Form structure

A form is made of a few parts, and each one has a single job.

Field (primal-field, .pr-field)
One question: its label, an optional description, the native control, and the message.
Label
The question, always visible and tied to its control, so clicking it focuses the control.
Description
What the person needs before they answer: the format, the limits, why it's asked. It's read out with the control.
Message
What went wrong and what to type, directly under the control. It's announced when it appears.
Group (primal-field-group, .pr-group)
Several controls that answer one question, under one legend: a radio group, an address, a date in three parts.
Form (form[primalForm])
The fields, then the actions a section gap below. On submit it shows every message and moves focus to the first one.
